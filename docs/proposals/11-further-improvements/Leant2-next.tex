\begin{abstract}
The original improvement plan began with measured Leant2 behavior at commit
\code{33cec8b}: saturated outcome-category regression suites, thirteen open
Church-operation stretch cases, sixteen edge probes, and a workload-specific
search profile. Those historical measurements remain identified by revision.
This revision integrates nine independently prepared expert-answer packages
into one design, rather than retaining nine overlapping answers to the same
31 questions.

The integrated design separates exact-query certification, conservative
pruning, bounded coverage, ranking, replay, and executable publication. It
retains constructive carrier and motive methods, corrected lower bounds,
typed partial-evaluation obligations, recursive realization and local proof
interfaces, dependent retrieval, and an explicit boundary for learned
proposals. Established answers replace the corresponding expert questions;
the active agenda asks narrower questions about the remaining formalization,
algorithmic and empirical gaps. A common roadmap and adversarial evaluation
plan connect the subsystems. Preserved Python model checks reproduce finite
examples and countermodels; they are not Lean proofs or new synthesis results.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Status.} This is a design document. Section~\ref{sec:evidence} preserves
the original measurements at \code{33cec8b}, not a fresh measurement of the
implementation. The expert packages inspected selected code at
\code{784664f}; their source-level observations are also historical. The
integration inventory pins all nine packages to repository revision
\code{5c3a53c}. New validation in this revision covers the preserved finite
Python suites and the consolidated documentation. It does not establish a
new Lean build, a formalization of the mathematical arguments, or an engine
speedup. Literature and version boundaries are recorded in the references
and integration ledger.
