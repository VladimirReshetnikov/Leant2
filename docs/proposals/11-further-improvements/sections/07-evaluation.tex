\section{R3. Evaluation of programs with holes}\label{sec:evaluation}

\subsection{The problem and revised boundary}\label{sec:evaluation-scope}

The proposal's measured starting point is repeated residual-contract evaluation:
substitute the partial program into an observation, reduce its decision
procedure, reject a decided false result, and continue when reduction is
stuck. The historical profile attributed 45\% of contract-query time to this
work; that is a workload measurement, not a prediction for the designs below.
Repeated folds restart; results and blockers are aggregated; backward demands
are unavailable; and unfinished recursive calls often block reduction.
Independent observations would serve search scheduling
(Section~\ref{sec:search}) and guard synthesis without changing the contract.

Responses N1--N9 correct the starting literature description. Smyth's simplified
formal core is first-order, but its implementation supports higher-order
examples and polymorphism, interdependent holes, and examples that are not
trace-complete\cite{smyth}. Its scope must not be reduced to its core exposition.
The research boundary is a dependency-correct adaptation to Lean. A correct
kernel primitive does not certify arbitrary preprocessing of an open term;
closed differential tests do not prove that partial-state pruning preserves
every completion. The guarantees and evidence categories of
Section~\ref{sec:semantic-boundaries} apply throughout.\prov{N1--N9}

\subsection{Typed closures and relational unevaluation}\label{sec:evaluation-closures}

Hazelnut Live supplies semantics with typed hole closures; Smyth resumes
computations and propagates required results backward. Scrybe supplies a later
example-propagation reference. Combinator-specific inversion in Myth and
$\lambda^2$, and the interpretation of examples as refinements, give further
implementation choices\cite{hazel,smyth,scrybe,myth,lambda2,frankle16}.
None supplies a ready-made conservative evaluator for all native Lean
expressions, universes, delayed assignments, and dependent conversion.

A hole declared $m:(\Delta\vdash T)$ is encountered in context $\Gamma$ through
a well-typed substitution $\rho:\Gamma\to\Delta$. Its closure records
\[
 H=(m,\Delta,\rho,T[\rho],\kappa,\nu),
\]
where $\kappa$ contains suspended eliminations or typed observations and $\nu$
identifies the immutable state version. All occurrences share one contextual
unknown. Evaluating $\mathsf{map}\ ?f\ [a,b]$ can preserve the list spine and
retain two closures for the same $f$, not two independently fillable holes.
A vector-producing hole observed at lengths zero and three has constraints in
different fibers. Preserve its substitutions, equality evidence, and transports;
erased lists or host-language equality cannot replace them. Proofs and
dictionaries can affect legal reduction even when proof arguments are erased
at runtime.\prov{N1--N9}

Higher-order examples are finite trees of demanded applications: a
function-valued result must satisfy a relation when applied to particular
typed arguments. Church inputs are observed through applications to eliminators.
This is not an extensional oracle at unobserved inputs, nor a reason to merge
functions that agree on the current table. An unobserved application stays
neutral unless another justified rule applies.

Unevaluation returns a relation on completions, not necessarily one value.
Constructor injectivity turns $\mathsf{cons}\ ?x\ ?xs=a::ys$ into necessary
field constraints. For noninjective $g$, $g(?x)=y$ requires the original
equation, a proved preimage rule, or alternatives covering all possibilities.
An unknown conditional yields both guard/branch alternatives; an application
with unknown function and argument may remain a joint constraint. Conditions
such as $f(0)=f(1)$ must preserve correlations between calls. Choosing one
sufficient explanation is useful guidance, but its failure refutes the branch
only if the alternatives collectively cover every satisfying completion.
\prov{N2,N3,N5,N6,N8,N9}

\paragraph{Certified observation extraction.}\label{sec:evaluation-observations}
For each extracted $O_i(p)$ retain $C(p)\to O_i(p)$ as in
Equation~\ref{eq:necessary-observation}. Refutation needs this direction;
acceptance by assembling observations also needs
$(\bigwedge_i O_i(p))\to C(p)$ or another reconstruction theorem.
Register justified decompositions of conjunctions, Boolean conjunction required
to be true, \code{decide} wrappers, and \code{List.all} over a fully known finite
list. A failed disjunct does not refute a disjunction. Infinite quantification
remains symbolic unless a sound reduction applies.\prov{N1--N9}

An arbitrary \code{BEq} dictionary is computational data. Replacing
\code{x == y = true} by $x=y$ requires the appropriate lawfulness theorem;
otherwise keep the exact Boolean test, including unconventional instances.
Multiple calls in a relational observation stay coupled. Give each observation
its own identity, decider when applicable, true/false/unknown status, residual,
and dependencies. New counterexamples add identities rather than silently
changing existing cache entries.

\paragraph{Fragment progression.}
Start with typed first-order observations on Booleans, naturals, lists, and
products; admit lambdas, applications, let bindings, constructors, projections,
known-constructor cases, selected constant-motive recursors, and named pure
primitives with checked semantics. Then add higher-order applications and
indexed constructors with explicit fibers, before arbitrary dependent
eliminators. Constructor disagreement in a closed prefix may refute while a
tail is open; a match blocked on an unknown index normally suspends.
Each extension needs substitution stability and rollback tests. Unsupported
inversions retain residual constraints or fall back; they never reinterpret a
value in the wrong fiber.\prov{N1--N9}

\subsection{Assignment-driven normalization and rollback}\label{sec:evaluation-cache}

Self-adjusting computation, Adapton, and named incremental computation give
methodology for demand tracking and \emph{from-scratch consistency}: reuse must
agree with reevaluation in the current branch. Canonical-min gives a more
direct control precedent, suspending constraints on metavariables, resuming on
assignment, and supporting push/pop search state. These results concern their
own calculi; they do not certify a full Lean normalizer. The reviewed
Canonical-min account leaves full mechanization as future work.
\cite{selfadjust,adapton,names,canonicalmin}\prov{N1--N9}

Use a residual computation DAG with immutable expression/closure nodes, values
or residuals, read sets, and dependency edges. Cache identity includes the
frozen observation, evaluator version, exact expression, local telescope and
substitution, environment, transparency/rewrite policy, and relevant mutable
reads. Reads include already assigned expression metavariables, universes,
delayed-assignment links, local declaration types and values, instances,
options, and equation/inversion lemmas whenever consulted. Final unassigned
blockers alone miss assigned values that chose a reduction branch. Dynamic
read sets may omit an unused branch of a known Boolean match and so be more
precise than whole-term syntactic dependencies.\prov{N1--N9}

\begin{proposition}[Read-set cache validity]\label{prop:evaluation-cache}
Suppose a deterministic evaluator has fixed immutable semantic inputs and
records a sound over-approximation of every mutable value read. If those inputs
and all recorded values agree in another state, its cached result agrees with
reevaluation there.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Replay the finite evaluation trace. Every read returns the same value, so the
same pure operation and next read are selected. Induction gives the same result
and termination point. An omitted dependency invalidates the hypothesis; an
extra dependency only causes unnecessary recomputation.
\end{proof}

The minimal counterexample evaluates $?m+1$ with $?m:=0$, rolls back, and assigns
$?m:=1$: the second answer is $2$, not the cached $1$. Equivalently, a false
result for $?m=1$ in the first branch must not reject the second. Both states
contain an assigned hole and may reuse a rolled-back numerical generation.
This ABA identity error is a model counterexample, not a reproduced Leant2
defect. Use immutable assignment nodes, persistent deltas, validated content
identities, or globally fresh stamps. A fresh global timestamp invalidating
everything is safe but sacrifices reuse. Hashes index entries; correctness-
sensitive collisions require structural confirmation.\prov{N1--N9}

Watchers enqueue affected observations on assignment. Rollback restores the
persistent branch-store root or revalidates read fingerprints; mutable watcher
lists must also restore or be indexed by branch lineage. Share closed
subexpressions in the frozen environment, and open residuals only through
validated closures. Historical inspections found \code{residualBlockers} in an
\code{IO.Ref} outside \code{Meta.SavedState}; that motivates ownership tests,
not an asserted false-result bug. In contrast, resource charges intentionally
survive rollback and are never refunded.

Cache unknown carefully: a stuck result is reusable only while its reads agree;
a timeout is a cost estimate, not evidence that a larger allowance cannot
decide it. Global false caching requires a certificate or an identity
establishing the same proposition, program, assumptions, and policy.
Contract-relative oracle facts must not become unconditional cache values.

Implement exact closed-result caching first, branch-local residuals next,
and cross-branch reuse only after assignment-version and delayed-substitution
tests. Measure hits, invalidations, recomputation, demand-queue work, closure
retention, and old-world memory. A large hit rate can still lose overall when
persistence retains too many environments.\prov{N4--N9}

\subsection{Checked evaluation before a new normalizer}\label{sec:evaluation-certification}

N4, N6, and N7 add an existing backend to the common small-fragment proposal:
benchmark Lean's proof-producing CBV machinery. The inspected Lean~4.34.0 source
exposes \code{Lean.Elab.Tactic.Cbv}, \code{evalDecideCbv}, \code{cbvGoal},
\code{cbvHyp}, and \code{cbvDecideGoal}. The \code{decide_cbv} wrapper applies
\code{of_decide_eq_true} and invokes the CBV goal checker. Equation-based
propositional rewriting handles useful closed observations and well-founded
definitions without code-generator trust of the kind used by
\code{native_decide}. Its proof dependencies still require the selected axiom
audit, and documented dependent-position limitations prevent treating it as
a complete open-hole evaluator. Source availability is not a performance or
compiled integration result.\cite{leancbv,leantactics}

Kernel conversion and proved equations are different specifications. A checked
rewriter may prove $e=v$ without conversion at the chosen strategy reducing
$e$ to $v$. This is useful semantic evidence, not an under-approximation of
conversion. If conversion is the specification, restrict the fragment to
kernel-convertible reductions. Compare current kernel reduction, ordinary
proof-producing simplification, CBV, and the custom fragment separately.

\begin{proposition}[A sufficient open-refutation certificate]\label{prop:evaluation-refutation}
Let a partial program $p$ be abstracted over a well-typed dependency-ordered
completion telescope $\Delta$, and let every allowed completion satisfy $D$.
A checked certificate
\[
 \forall\theta:\Delta,\quad D(\theta)\to\neg C(p[\theta])
\]
justifies removing the branch. Additional assumptions are permitted only when
every contract-satisfying completion entails them.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A hypothetical accepted completion supplies its typed telescope values and
the required assumptions, yielding a contradiction. In the conditional case,
the hypothesis that it satisfies $C$ supplies the additional assumptions.
\end{proof}
A Boolean certificate
$\mathsf{decide}(O(p[\theta]))=\mathsf{false}$ for a necessary observation is
one instance. A proof about one arbitrary filling rejects only that filling;
successful simulation of closed programs is weaker than this open obligation.
This instantiates Equation~\ref{eq:conservative-pruning}.\prov{N1--N9}

A \emph{substitution-stable reducer} proves that each permitted reduction
commutes with every well-typed completion; substituting into a false-ending
trace preserves false. An \emph{abstract evaluator} instead proves
\[
 \{\llbracket O(p[\theta])\rrbracket:\theta\text{ allowed}\}
 \subseteq\gamma(\widehat{\mathsf{eval}}(O,p)).
\]
If this over-approximation has no permitted outcome, the branch is refuted.
Under-approximating possible behaviors is unsafe for exclusion.
Under-approximating \emph{proved judgments} is safe: more answers are unknown,
while each reported decision still has evidence.

The reports identify a specific proof boundary in \code{instantiatePartial}:
its reduction-only delayed-assignment expansion permits intermediate
metavariables whose local contexts no longer match. This is not a demonstrated
counterexample, but it prevents assuming ordinary well-typed substitution.
Carry typed substitution spines or prove a relation from that representation
to every admissible native completion. A correct \code{Kernel.whnf} invocation
alone cannot establish the preprocessing relation.\prov{N1--N7,N9}

\paragraph{Deployment alternatives.}\label{sec:evaluation-deployment}
First let fast custom evaluation change priority only. Next let it propose
compact negative evidence checked before a prune. Finally prove a typed object
language's denotation and fuelled interpreter, or a verified abstract
transformer. A proof-producing reducer composes step equalities and integrates
incrementally; a reflected evaluator uses a shared soundness theorem and may
lower checking overhead after a larger initial proof investment. Compact
traces can be checked when committing a prune and certified summaries cached.
These remain alternative engineering choices, not measured superiority claims.
\prov{N1--N9}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Proposed evidence-bearing evaluation interface; pseudocode.}]
EvalResult :=
  value      (typedValue, equalityEvidence, readSet)
| suspended  (typedClosure, blockers, readSet)
| rejectHint (diagnostic, readSet)
| refuted    (checkedBranchCertificate, assumptions, readSet)
| resourceExhausted
\end{lstlisting}
Only \code{refuted} removes a branch in the conservative lane. Unsupported
syntax, ill-scoped residuals, unproved side conditions, exhausted fuel, and
failed certificate construction give unknown or propagate resource interruption,
never false. Later acceptance checking cannot recover an earlier unsound prune.

Lean4Lean supplies a checker and mechanized metatheory, with verification work
extending over time. It supplies identified definitions and proved lemmas,
not automatic extraction of every later kernel optimization into a verified
incremental evaluator. A new closure machine still needs its environment
relation, substitution lemmas, and simulation theorem.
\cite{lean4lean,lean4lean2026}

\subsection{Angelic calls and the bounded guarantee}\label{sec:evaluation-angelic}

Angelic calls are existential results constrained by the specification. A sound
relaxation admits every ordinary execution of every allowed completion.
Unsatisfiability can then refute; a satisfying angelic execution is merely
permission to continue. A finite list of guessed outputs under-approximates
behavior unless it covers every realization. One failed guess, incomplete
solving, or a timeout cannot establish universal failure.\prov{N1--N9}

Call identities include the function or recursion frame, full typed argument
tuple, implicit computational parameters, universes, and relevant environment.
Known-equal calls share outputs or compatibility constraints in the appropriate
fiber. Independent angels can satisfy $f(0)\ne f(0)$: this is a loose
over-approximation, not itself an unsound refutation, but cannot count as an
actual program. Imposing equality on unequal calls, or silently bounding an
infinite result domain, may instead remove a real completion. Syntactically
different symbolic inputs are not necessarily unequal.

If $C(f)\to f(v)=w$ is established, the oracle may use $w$ under $C$.
A contradiction excludes contract-satisfying completions, not necessarily every
arbitrary completion. Retain the implication in certificates and cache
identities. If several outputs are permitted, retain their relation.
Unobserved calls may remain unconstrained. Observations of $f(s)=g(s,a_0)$
do not specify the invented accumulator helper $g(s,a)$ at arbitrary $a$
(Section~\ref{sec:recursion-angels}).

Burst contributes strengthening, ordinary candidate checks, and backtracking;
Smyth contributes a different live-constraint organization. Their guarantees
do not transfer merely by changing search direction\cite{burst,smyth}.
The responses' bounded claims can be stated once as follows.

\begin{theorem}[Finite checked refinement]\label{thm:evaluation-bounded}
Fix a finite explicitly represented set $F$ of well-typed concrete candidates,
including bounds on bodies, literals, universes, helpers, instances, recursion
schemes, and proof plans. Suppose validation terminates and decides the stated
observations, selection is fair, refinements preserve every satisfying member,
and each unsuccessful completed iteration removes its selected failing
candidate without indefinitely repeating unresolved auxiliary states.
Then refinement terminates with a satisfying member or correctly exhausts $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Satisfying members are never removed. Every unsuccessful completed iteration
strictly decreases the finite remaining set, so at most $|F|$ such iterations
occur. Fair terminating selection and validation eventually recognize a
satisfying member or exhaust the set.
\end{proof}

Another sufficient implementation bounds candidate--oracle pairs, uses an
exact terminating constraint solver and well-founded recursive calls, and
strictly removes an incompatible pair or failed candidate on each refinement.
Finite syntax alone does not bound integer outputs, fresh calls, helper
declarations, or refinement sequences. Incomplete unification, top-$k$ retrieval
without fallback, and proof attempts indefinitely returning unknown prevent
exhaustion claims. An expanding grammar with fair dovetailing supports only a
relative semidecision guarantee; fuel or wall-clock exhaustion stays unknown.

\subsection{Validation and new questions}\label{sec:evaluation-checks}

The implementation sequence is certified observation extraction, immutable
reports and watchers, closed proof-producing backends, a typed fragment with
checked negatives, then oracle constraints with certified pruning. Angelic
guidance alone is a useful earlier milestone. A small bottom-up pool of typed
closed realizations can supplement top-down holes; behavioral summaries index
alternatives and new observations split or regenerate buckets instead of
permanently discarding their contents.

Regressions must include open terms under multiple completions, repeated hole
applications, universes, delayed assignments, indexed constructors, casts,
proof-dependent types, local dictionaries, transitive reads, exceptions,
sibling rollback, and reuse of conditional facts outside their assumptions.
Compare cached and fresh evaluation. Closed generated-term differential
testing supplements these checks; even $10^5$ agreements do not prove the
completion theorem. Charge certificate generation/checking and recomputation:
a fast hint followed by a full replay at every node need not save time.
The submitted Python cache/oracle models illustrate failure modes; they are
neither Lean verification nor engine performance measurements.\prov{N1--N9}

The original four questions are answered above at their stated boundaries.
The remaining research questions are more specific:
\begin{enumerate}[label=R3.N\arabic*.]
\item What is the smallest typed residual fragment covering the measured hot
observations for which substitution stability, delayed-assignment closure,
and a proof-producing refutation path can all be established?
\item Which complete read-set representation gives useful cross-branch reuse
without retaining excessive old environments, and can its from-scratch
consistency be proved for the actual Lean state interface?
\item Under each permitted axiom profile, when do kernel reduction, equation-
based CBV, reflected evaluation, and checked speculative traces improve
end-to-end observation cost, including certificate construction and checking?
\item Which finite relational oracle language preserves all bounded concrete
recursive behaviors while supporting useful complete refutation, especially
for dependent fibers and invented helpers outside their initial seed?
\end{enumerate}
