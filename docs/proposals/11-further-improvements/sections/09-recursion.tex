\section{R5. General recursion from incomplete examples}\label{sec:recursion}

\subsection{The missing capability and revised comparisons}\label{sec:recursion-scope}

The historical implementation admitted outermost constant-motive recursion on
an index-free recursive inductive other than \code{Nat}, exposing induction
hypotheses as its recursive calls. Its list-length/map and tree-size successes
do not establish natural recursion, indexed motives, changing parameters,
derived measures, helper invention, or mutual/nested recursion. The intended
end state retains these requirements. A small scheme portfolio specifies an
implementation order, not a replacement for them.

Several examples need a corrected diagnosis. Zip can recurse on one list while
generalizing and matching the other. Fibonacci can use a pair of successive
values under ordinary natural recursion rather than necessarily requiring
sub-subterm calls. Tree flattening can use append, an accumulator, or a
difference-list carrier; with append already provided, body depth and search
order may dominate. Merge admits nested structural organization or a measure
on the joint argument tuple. These alternatives separate expressibility,
representation cost, and discovery difficulty.\prov{N1--N9}

Lean's definition elaborators, recursor metadata, equation theorems, and
termination principles provide realization infrastructure. Size-change and
lexicographic analysis supply established termination methodology, but the
synthesizer still proposes calls and discharges decrease obligations
\cite{sizechange}. The historical estimate of tens of milliseconds per
elaborated candidate motivates measuring and amortizing realization; it is
not a demonstrated universal cost bound.

Myth uses recursive examples in a trace-complete regime. Smyth explicitly
supports interdependent holes and sparse recursive traces; grouping it under
a blanket trace-completeness requirement was incorrect. Burst supplies angelic
execution and strengthening, Trio builds recursive programs from nonrecursive
expressions, and Synquid uses refinement/measure information. These are
different synthesis and validation settings, not transferable Leant2 scores
\cite{myth,smyth,burst,trio,synquid}. The mixed-quantifier recursive-sketch and
counterexample-derived syntactic constraints of the July~2026 Cataclyst
preprint are another comparison point, not a dependent Lean implementation
or a ready-checked pruning rule.\cite{cataclyst}\prov{N1,N3--N9}

\subsection{Typed capabilities and explicit realization}\label{sec:recursion-capabilities}

Retain the unified proposal's recursion plan: function telescope, selected
major argument, fixed/generalized parameters, motive, branch telescopes and
index equations, scoped recursive capabilities, program/proof holes,
termination obligations, final specialization, and executable lowering.
Search may use equation-like bodies while the kernel checks realized recursor
or well-founded terms. Neither representation alone proves their agreement.

Let $X$ contain the complete recursive state, $B:X\to\Sort\,u$ be the result
family, and $R:X\to X\to\Prop$ a chosen well-founded relation. At state $x$,
the capability is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:recursion-capability}
 \mathit{rec}_x:(y:X)\to R(y,x)\to B(y).
\end{equation}
The result is $B(y)$, not a fixed type or $B(x)$. Changing accumulators, other
arguments, and mutual-recursion tags belong to $X$ or are explicitly quantified
in the result family. A measure may depend on only part of $X$, but call
identity and admissibility use the full state. Otherwise unequal calls can be
conflated and the indexed programs of Section~\ref{sec:dependent} excluded.
\prov{N1--N9}

The body has no unrestricted provisional self-reference. Capabilities are
scoped to a recursion frame and cannot leak into ordinary providers.
Structural constructor paths cheaply supply some decrease evidence; measure
calls create proof obligations. Decrease on one sample is not a universal
proof. An unresolved proof hole may remain a guarded obligation, but deductions
depending on it stay heuristic until their hypotheses hold. Never introduce a
global temporary axiom naming the recursive function and overlook it during
the dependency audit.

A typed body functional has type
\[
 F:\prod_{x:X}
       \left(\left(\prod_{y:X}R(y,x)\to B(y)\right)\to B(x)\right).
\]
Realization produces $f:\prod_x B(x)$ and a checked equation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:recursion-equation}
 f(x)=F\bigl(x,\lambda y\,h.\ f(y)\bigr),
\end{equation}
including administrative parameters and casts of the chosen encoding.
Keep ordinary expressions and types native to Lean; the additional IR need
only encode recursive-call structure, guards, branch parameters, shared holes,
and pending termination obligations.\prov{N2,N5,N6,N9}

Two realization paths remain useful. Send promising closed typed bodies
through Lean's equation elaborator and retain declarations/equations; or
instantiate a small library of already certified recursive templates. The
second can avoid repeated full elaboration but must not become an unverified
parallel termination compiler. Realize selected survivors or reusable helpers,
cache checked equations, and charge realization/certification to the reported
end-to-end budget. Generated declarations, equations, and termination evidence
extend the environment fingerprint and acceptance audit.

\subsection{Interpreter agreement is generally propositional}\label{sec:recursion-simulation}

Structural encodings can have convenient definitional equations. General
well-founded definitions often require propositions such as
\code{WellFounded.fix_eq}; Lean documents examples whose expected equation
does not close by \code{rfl}. The opposite blanket claim is also too strong:
N5 and N8 identify Lean~4.27's reducible fuel-based implementation for relevant
natural-measure definitions. State the encoding and pinned-version behavior
rather than asserting that every well-founded equation is only propositional.
\cite{leanrecursion,lean427}\prov{N1--N9}

\begin{proposition}[Interpreter--realizer agreement]\label{prop:recursion-simulation}
Suppose a typed body interpreter and realization use the same body semantics,
every call carries valid $R(y,x)$ evidence, nonrecursive operations preserve
their intended dependent meanings, and the realization satisfies
Equation~\ref{eq:recursion-equation}. Every successful finite interpreter
evaluation at $x$ returning $v:B(x)$ establishes $f(x)=v$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use well-founded induction on $x$. The decrease proof of each call $y$ makes
the induction hypothesis available, identifying its interpreted result with
$f(y)$. Substitute these equalities into the proved semantics of the
nonrecursive operations, using the same dependent substitutions and transports.
The realization's equation identifies the resulting value with $f(x)$.
Equivalently, induct on a successful finite evaluation derivation explicitly
recording all recursive calls.
\end{proof}

Equality preservation is an obligation, not a consequence of matching
input/output types. For reflected bodies, prove it by induction on syntax.
If the proof equates higher-order recursive oracles using extensionality,
account for that principle in the axiom profile. The equivalent uniqueness
formulation says two total interpretations satisfying the same well-founded
equations agree pointwise when the body respects pointwise equality of
recursive results.\prov{N2,N3,N9}

A fuelled interface records a derivation or checked trace and satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:recursion-fuel}
 \mathsf{runFuel}(k,b,x)=\mathsf{some}(v)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathsf{realize}(b)(x)=v.
\end{equation}
Fuel exhaustion gives unknown, neither divergence nor a bad contract value.
Adequate-fuel/eventual-success is a separate theorem for a declared total
fragment. A decreasing natural measure may bound recursion depth, not every
reduction or branching evaluation-tree size; define the fuel unit. Termination
does not supply a small uniform interactive-budget bound. Simulation of closed
programs also does not justify open pruning without the all-completions
condition of Section~\ref{sec:evaluation-certification}.\prov{N1--N9}

For closed observations, rewrite with generated equations, use proof-producing
simplification or \code{cbv}/\code{decide_cbv}, or evaluate a reified body and
reconstruct the observation proof through Equation~\ref{eq:recursion-fuel}.
Benchmark native equation evaluation before a universal interpreter.
Keep three trust profiles distinct: direct kernel reduction; proof-producing
interpretation checked under the original axioms; and compiled computation
with explicitly approved additional assumptions. Compiled execution can screen,
rank, or nominate counterexamples; kernel-only hard pruning needs checked
negative evidence. The source reports identify native reduction axioms such as
\code{Lean.ofReduceBool} as excluded providers, so quietly using them changes
the policy.\cite{leancbv,leantactics}\prov{N1,N4,N6,N7,N9}

\subsection{Publishing an executable definition}\label{sec:recursion-publication}

The recorded code-generator failure on a logically accepted recursor term is
an independent boundary. Preserve the logical object, construct a compilable
equation-style definition, and prove pointwise correspondence or reprove the
original contract for that definition. Attaching \code{implemented_by} is not
an equality proof.

Lean's \code{Nat.recCompiled}, \code{Nat.rec_eq_recCompiled}, and its
\code{@[csimp]} attribute supply a proof-backed compilation-rewrite precedent
in the pinned core. Its comment identifies recursor compiler support as the
motivation. Investigate a small publication adapter using this pattern before
a universal recursor decompiler.\cite{leannat}\prov{N3,N5}

The bridge proof may introduce axioms absent from the logical term, especially
through extensionality; audit it separately. A pointwise bridge or direct
contract proof may avoid an unnecessarily strong equality obligation.
Logical equivalence does not itself verify the compiler/runtime, and
unaccounted implementation substitutions remain relevant. Record kernel
acceptance, contract certification, export, compilation, and representative
execution separately. A noncompiling logical result remains useful with
explicit status; a compiling substitute lacking the bridge is not automatically
the certified program. Include publication work in time to usable result.\cite{leancompile,leanvalidation}
\prov{N1--N9}

For an invented helper bundle, construct its declaration-dependency graph
and check an acyclic bundle in topological order. Each declaration must close
over the advertised telescope, depend only on imported or earlier accepted
declarations, preserve its universe parameters, and satisfy the transitive
axiom policy. Recheck the final program and contract in the exported bundle;
scratch-environment success is insufficient. Mutual recursion requires an
explicitly supported bundle rule with its own joint termination and
realization checks, rather than bypassing the acyclic audit.\prov{N5}

\subsection{Top-down angels, sharing, and helper invariants}\label{sec:recursion-angels}

Section~\ref{sec:evaluation-angelic} states the oracle semantics and finite
theorem once. Top-down search gains expected types, local telescopes, induction
information, and sketches. Bottom-up version spaces or finite-tree automata
share completed subterms and behaviors more directly. Neither dominates without
a matched experiment, and changing direction does not inherit Burst's space
savings or correctness proof.\prov{N1--N9}

A branch carries its partial equation body and a persistent call-constraint
graph. A descriptor includes frame/function identity, full typed arguments,
universes, local substitution, and assumptions. Hash-cons with checked identity.
Filling constructor fields propagates demands; filling guards selects
conditional constraints; refining arguments can identify a call with a known
input or another call. Assignment watchers invalidate both forward residuals
and affected oracle assumptions. Repeated occurrences of a hole remain one
shared unknown.

Keep three evidence levels: answers forced by the original contract;
existential answers that could make the sketch pass; and actual results of
the closed realized candidate. The first constrain accepted completions, the
second guide or enter sound relational solving, and the third support final
checking. From $r_1+r_2=5$, committing to $0,5$ and discarding other explanations
is invalid. If both symbols denote the same call, include their equality.
If one observation permits zero or one and another demands positivity,
prematurely choosing zero loses the feasible option.\prov{N2,N6}

When execution contradicts a guessed result, reject the completed body or
refine that combination of skeleton and assumptions. A failed unobserved-call
guess does not ban all bodies or globally ban the input. Nogoods retain exactly
their supporting assumptions; specification-relative counterexample caches
are not universal facts about every candidate.\prov{N4,N7,N8}

For an invented worker $g(s,a)$ with $f(s)=g(s,a_0)$, observations of $f$
normally constrain only seed $a_0$. Reusing them at arbitrary $a$ is invalid.
Supply a generalized invariant, derived typed examples, or concrete execution.
The reversal invariant
$g(xs,acc)=\mathsf{reverse}(xs)\mathbin{+\!+}acc$, universally quantified over
$acc$, illustrates joint generalization of a changed parameter and its
correctness property. Helper/carrier alternatives remain reopenable when a
proposed strengthening is unsupported.\prov{N1,N5,N9}

Use a hybrid: shallow closed components for a specific telescope and target,
typed recipes retained behind behavioral summaries, offered to top-down
refinement. Cross-context reuse needs checked renaming/substitution. A behavior
vector is an index, not a permanent quotient: new recursive inputs may split
its bucket. Begin with observed calls, then symbolic unknown calls for ordering,
then a complete certified relational fragment for pruning. Execute the finished
recursion ordinarily and check the original full contract with no remaining
angels. Convenient assumptions do not fabricate trace-complete examples.
\prov{N1--N9}

\subsection{A staged recursion portfolio and its limits}\label{sec:recursion-basis}

No response establishes a canonical minimal basis or Leant2 coverage percentage.
A universal well-founded recursor can make a basis syntactically tiny while
hiding difficult measure, algebra, and proof searches. Optimize coverage at
bounded total cost under a fixed component inventory, including scheme
selection, body filling, decrease proofs, realization, and certification.

Paramorphisms are a useful published starting point. For a polynomial functor
$F$, an algebra $F(\mu F\times K)\to K$ sees each original child and recursive
result; a fold ignores the original child. For lists,
\[
 \mathsf{para}(z,s,[])=z,\qquad
 \mathsf{para}(z,s,a::xs)=s(a,xs,\mathsf{para}(z,s,xs)).
\]
Natural primitive recursion similarly exposes predecessor and prior result.
Para uses typed multi-hole templates with recursion-free fillers and reports
a 59-benchmark study. Its non-kernel validation and resource setting do not
establish Leant2's ten-second dependent-synthesis coverage.\cite{para}
\prov{N1,N8}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{.23\linewidth}L{.69\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Tier & Purpose, witness, and additional obligation\\
\midrule\endhead
Structural &
Registered folds/paramorphisms, including naturals and supported indexed
families: map, length, tree size, powers. Validate motives and
constructor-field capabilities.\\
Generalized/stateful &
Changing parameters, function accumulators, option-like empty state, products,
dependent result strengthening. Reverse, zip on one structural list, extrema,
Fibonacci pairs, and difference-list flattening need their initializer,
updater, and finisher.\\
Nested/helper &
Bounded nesting over other arguments/substructures; initially one synthesized
helper with a closed parameter interface, without hidden access to a parent's
changing context.\\
Course-of-values &
Certified deeper-predecessor access when structural/product encodings are
unsuitable. Record constructor paths, indices, and admitted calls; depth two
is a syntactic bound, not all course-of-values coverage.\\
Measured &
Finite measure/lexicographic grammar on full states, checked well-foundedness
and per-call decrease. Bound arithmetic expressions, tuple lengths, relation
choices, and decrease-proof search.\\
Mutual/nested families &
Later adapters using actual recursor metadata, tagged joint states or generated
definition bundles, and their real induction/termination interfaces; avoid
brittle generated-name rules.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
Rows overlap: count all admissible reference encodings and measure the cheapest
verified one. Task names do not determine necessary schemes. Providers can
hide substantial recursion, so report their capabilities alongside visible
template complexity.\prov{N1--N9}

The measured tier includes merge with sum of remaining lengths; Euclidean gcd
with a nonzero-branch modulus-decrease theorem; division/binary search with
decreasing intervals; and quicksort with filtering-length bounds. Calling
\code{omega} does not supply every semantic lemma. \code{sizeOf}, length,
natural arguments, bounded differences, and lexicographic tuples require
precise semantics and proof plans. A failed measure/proof attempt rejects
that proposal, not the original problem. Prefer measures whose obligations
lie in a declared proof fragment before broad invention.\prov{N1,N2,N5--N9}

When body search reaches a consistently constrained hole beyond its depth,
propose a helper with a dependency-closed parameter interface and derived
necessary observations. Do not assume those observations fully specify it.
Retain caller reconstruction and the original final contract. One helper per
query is an experiment, not an expressiveness theorem. Mutual/nested inductives
need dedicated tracks. Coinductive or intentionally partial programs require
a different semantic acceptance profile; interpreter fuel must not quietly
admit them into a total-function claim.\prov{N1,N5,N7,N8}

\subsection{Coverage experiments and new questions}\label{sec:recursion-evaluation}

First translate a withheld reference witness into the declared scheme grammar
and kernel-check it: this establishes representation coverage. Next run with
scheme and carrier supplied, scheme alone, and neither. Distinguish providers
enabled/withheld and disclose helpers. These separate body search, carrier
invention, scheme choice, and retrieval. A handwritten witness is neither
discovery nor timing evidence.\prov{N1--N9}

Stratify list/natural/tree exercises, unresolved Church/indexed cases, and
adversarial variations of index expressions, parameter order, and termination
measures. Hold out families when assessing guidance. Compare trace-complete
examples, sparse examples with neutral calls, and sparse examples with angels
at matched body/scheme bounds. Include a case omitting all internal calls and
require actual realized execution to decide acceptance. Record overlapping
scheme encodings before attributing marginal coverage to one tier.

Report first sufficient/successful schemes, carriers, components, helpers,
trace completeness, universal-proof requirements, decrease-proof costs,
realization, kernel checking, compilation, and execution. Distinguish
out-of-grammar, interrupted search, unresolved decrease, behavioral counterexample,
and unresolved contract proof. Give logical-work and wall-clock curves through
the usable exported artifact. Original ten-second recursion probes and
regression scores remain gates; a toy model or supplied-scheme success does
not prove automatic invention at that budget.\prov{N1--N9}

The original interpreter, direction, and basis questions have the conditional
answers above. Remaining questions are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=R5.N\arabic*.]
\item Which minimal typed recursive-body IR supports checked simulation into
native equation elaboration and reusable templates, including dependent
results, and what adequate-fuel theorem is practical for that fragment?
\item How should a persistent top-down oracle store represent helper invariants
and assumption-scoped nogoods so refinements retain all alternatives while
recovering useful bottom-up sharing?
\item Which recursion portfolio and measure/proof grammar give the best verified
coverage when representation, supplied-scheme filling, automatic invention,
and executable publication are measured separately?
\item Can a small proof-backed publication adapter, using equation definitions
or compiler simplification theorems, preserve each permitted axiom profile
while making the synthesized recursor families executable?
\end{enumerate}
