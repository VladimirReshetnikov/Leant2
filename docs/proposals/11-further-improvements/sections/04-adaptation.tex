\section{Adaptation items}\label{sec:adaptation}

These techniques have precedents in other settings; their guarantees do not
transfer automatically to dependent Lean synthesis. Gap descriptions below
refer to the original \code{33cec8b} planning snapshot. The revised adaptations
state the extra conditions required by the integrated answers, rather than
claiming an inventory of currently missing features.

\subsection{A1. Conditionals and guard abduction}

\paragraph{Historical motivation.} The original grammar lacked comparison
components and a Boolean/decidable guard rule for terms such as
\code{if a <= b then b else a}. Probes 9 and 10 motivated this extension.
Adding a missing production removes that particular expressibility obstacle;
it does not establish that the extended search will find and certify a result.

\paragraph{What is known.} Enumerative synthesizers separate \emph{terms} from \emph{conditions}: find terms that are each correct on some examples, then learn a decision tree of guards that routes every example to a correct term (the divide-and-conquer strategy of EUSolver~\cite{eusolver}). Type-directed synthesizers abduce the guard instead: Synquid generates a branch, asks a solver for the weakest condition under which it satisfies the refinement type, and synthesizes a guard that implies it~\cite{synquid}. Example-directed tools split on a scrutinee when the examples disagree about the result~\cite{myth}.

\paragraph{Adaptation.} For a supported observation fragment, extend residual
evaluation to report which observations a typed branch candidate satisfies,
with its local input environment, instead of returning only the first failure.
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item collect branch candidates whose satisfied sets cover all observations;
\item enumerate guards from a small grammar over the locals (decidable comparisons of locals of the same type, Boolean-valued locals and their applications, \code{isEmpty}-style tests obtained from constructors);
\item evaluate each guard on those typed input environments and search a bounded
decision-tree grammar for a covering tree; call it smallest only after complete
search under a fixed cost and bound;
\item emit \code{if g then t1 else t2} (or \code{match}), and let the ordinary closed-program check certify it.
\end{enumerate}
The difficult adaptation is identifying ``the inputs of an observation.'' A
contract can relate several calls to $f$ or invoke it under a fold. A trace
must retain their correlations; routing each call independently because a
whole conjunct held for one constant branch candidate does not preserve that
conjunct after branch mixing. Use checked observation decomposition, or keep
the relational constraint and validate the assembled decision tree against
the original contract. Necessary inferred observations need the implication
in Section~\ref{sec:semantic-boundaries}; unknown decomposition remains opaque.
Recursive call environments add the capability and constraint obligations in
Sections~\ref{sec:evaluation} and~\ref{sec:recursion}.

\paragraph{Closing test.} Probes 9 and 10; the \code{max}, \code{min}, \code{filter}, \code{insert-sorted} and \code{compare} entries of the ported benchmarks.

\subsection{A2. Recursion on natural numbers, literals and arithmetic}

\paragraph{Historical motivation.} The original plan excluded natural-number
structural recursion after nested induction caused search growth, and listed
only \code{Nat.add}, \code{Nat.succ} and \code{Nat.zero} as numeric components.
Its probe 4 ($2^n$) therefore motivated both recursion and arithmetic extensions.
This is a statement about that restricted planning grammar, not current
implementation coverage or mathematical nonexistence.

\paragraph{Adaptation.} Keep three choices separate. First, prioritize an
outermost \code{Nat.rec} schema with explicit decreasing capabilities before
nested recursion; an outermost-only tier is a useful restriction, not coverage
of every primitive-recursive program. Second, seed literal enumeration with
constants from the contract plus 0, 1 and 2, charging literal encoding size.
Broader bounds must eventually admit omitted literals if relative coverage is
claimed. Third, evaluate demand-directed \code{Nat.mul}, \code{Nat.sub},
\code{Nat.pred} and comparison components while preserving natural-number
truncated-subtraction semantics. Carrier, motive and recursion bounds belong
to the joint grammar of Section~\ref{sec:carrier-motives}.

Measure native reduction on closed numerals and partially known arithmetic
separately. An unresolved term may block an optimized reduction; that alone
does not establish that every nearby hole forces unary evaluation or identify
the dominant cost. Use residual traces to justify any specialized evaluator
and preserve its unknown outcomes and arithmetic semantics.

\paragraph{Closing test.} Probe 4, factorial, Fibonacci with a pair accumulator (which needs R2), sum to $n$, and the \code{nat_*} entries of the Myth suite.

\subsection{A3. Normal forms and symmetry reduction}

\paragraph{Historical motivation.} The original traces showed duplicate
construction paths, administrative $\beta\eta$ variants and redundant fold
steps. These motivate canonical construction and duplicate detection, but a
well-typed simplification and a behavior-preserving search quotient have
different obligations. For example, an element-ignoring step may yield the
seed; it must not be called the identity on the input list without checking
the entire fold and its type.

\paragraph{What is known.} Focused inhabitation calculi use canonical
$\beta$-normal $\eta$-long forms for their specified calculi; Canonical is a
relevant dependent-search reference~\cite{canonical}. Applying that statement
to an unrestricted Lean grammar additionally requires accounting for
definitional reductions, proof arguments and supported syntax. Bottom-up
synthesizers use finite observational signatures to compress component spaces
\cite{transit,escher}; their interchangeability claim is relative to the
observation environments and compositional language, not arbitrary contexts.

\paragraph{Adaptation.} Audit overlapping exact-local and introduction routes
and redundant forward applications, then normalize only within a documented
typed fragment. A restriction that removes forward applications needs either
a completeness argument for the chosen normal-form grammar or an explicit
incompleteness label. Symmetry reduction for a commutative component requires
its actual commutativity law and a representative policy consistent with the
candidate objective.

Finite signatures are safe hard quotients only when equivalent representatives
remain interchangeable in \emph{all admitted surrounding contexts}, or when
discarded alternatives can be recovered by the reference search. Ground type
and finitely instantiated free variables alone do not prove that congruence:
a higher-order or recursive context can invoke the fragment in an unobserved
environment. Keep observational buckets as ranking/proposal structures by default and use checked
equality or a certified fragment theorem for permanent merging. The same
restriction applies to the bottom-up companion of Section~\ref{sec:search}.

\subsection{A4. Parametricity-directed test inputs}

\paragraph{Conditional idea.} In an explicitly parametric fragment, a family
\code{f : (A : Type) -> List A -> List A} commutes with \code{map}; the surface
type alone does not prove this for arbitrary Lean providers or classical
constructions. Theorem~\ref{thm:carrier-containers} and
Equation~\ref{eq:carrier-position-test} derive the shape/position reduction,
including repeated elements, empty types and its universe assumptions.
The polymorphic-testing literature supplies its own relational hypotheses
\cite{wadler89,bernardy10}; a theorem about a suitable monomorphic instance
does not make finitely many values of that instance globally complete tests.

\paragraph{Use in Leant2.} Explain a candidate with a symbolic example such as
\code{[0,1,2] |-> [2,1,0]}, and show a distinguishing input when accepted
candidates disagree. Group equal sampled behaviors with an explicit sample
scope; do not call them equal programs. Under proved naturality, agreement on
canonical inputs through length $N$ proves equality only on inputs through
length $N$. Equality for every program of a syntax-size bound would require a
separate small-model theorem, which the incoming reports do not establish.

Hard deduplication needs that stronger justification or checked candidate
equality. Equality/order dictionaries, opaque callbacks and indexed fibers
require provider-specific preservation laws and suitable symbolic structures;
see Section~\ref{sec:carrier-parametricity}. Use canonical tests as useful
probes immediately, and claim complete reduction only for a certified fragment.

\subsection{A5. Rule indexing}

The historical repeated local/provider scans motivate Lean's \code{DiscrTree}
or \code{LazyDiscrTree}, with Aesop as a rule-selection reference~\cite{aesop,aesopreadme}.
These indexes can avoid scanning unrelated rigid heads, but retrieval cost also
depends on matching branches, wildcard entries and returned candidates. It is
not bounded solely by goal size independent of the index or output size.

Index provider conclusions for a pinned environment and normalize keys under
an explicit transparency policy. Maintain local entries with valid dependency
identities as contexts and assignments change. Persistent structure permits
sharing; placing it inside the actual branch snapshot or validating its keys
is still necessary for rollback. Bare-variable conclusions require broad
matches, and reductions or transports accepted by the native checker must not
be silently excluded by stricter keys. Demand filtering is a heuristic unless
its necessary-condition argument is proved. Section~\ref{sec:retrieval}
states the dependent and dictionary obligations for stronger filtering.

\paragraph{Closing test.} Retain the original target of less than 10\% node-time
growth with two thousand session declarations as a hypothesis to measure.
Report cold index construction, warm retrieval, wildcard density, returned
candidate count and native checking separately; verify retrieval recall on
known usable components rather than measuring speed alone.
