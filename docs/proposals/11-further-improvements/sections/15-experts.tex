\section{Where outside expertise would help}\label{sec:experts}

The questions of Part~III are collected here in the order in which answers would change the plan most. For each we name the projects and fields whose research addresses the question, and what we would bring to a conversation, because a specialist's time is best spent on a small, concrete artifact rather than on a description.

\paragraph{Venues.} The Lean community chat is the natural first venue for questions 2, 4, 5 and 6, which concern Lean's own elaborator, kernel and automation. The others are questions for the program-synthesis and programming-languages research communities, reachable through the projects' public repositories and the cited papers.

\paragraph{Order of contact.} Question 1 first, because it decides whether R2 is a research project or an engineering one, and everything in P5 waits on it. Questions 2 and 3 next, because they shape the P2 and P4 rewrites of the search core and the evaluator, which are expensive to redo. The others can wait for their phases.

\paragraph{What we do not need help with.} Parts~I and~II. The items there are understood, their tests are written down, and the measurements of Section~\ref{sec:evidence} say in which order to do them.
