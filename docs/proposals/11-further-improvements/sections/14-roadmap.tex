\subsection{Phases and gates}

Every phase ends with the six harnesses at full score for 10 s and 5 s budgets, plus its own gate.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{14mm}L{62mm}L{72mm}@{}}
\toprule
Phase & Content & Gate\\
\midrule
\endhead
P1 & E8 benchmark port; E1 compilable results; E4 profiling and per-local caching; R3 stage 1 (per-observation report) & benchmark baseline recorded (expected: most recursion and conditional entries fail); probe 6 binds cleanly; node cost under 1 ms on Church probes\\
P2 & R1 steps 1--2 (first-class states, cost order); A5 indexing; A3 normal-form checks; E5 parallel lanes & identical candidate lists to the depth-first engine on the whole corpus; no repeated passes; uninhabited queries answered in lane time\\
P3 & A1 guards; A2 \code{Nat} recursion and literals; E7 portfolio and bounded instantiation; E6 propositional decision procedure & probes 4, 9, 10; conditional and arithmetic benchmark entries; propositional queries certified both ways\\
P4 & R3 stages 2--3; R1 steps 3--4 (learned costs, goal order by domain size) & differential agreement with the kernel on every generated closed program; solved count at 1 s improves on P2\\
P5 & R2 carrier grammar, carrier refutation, observation-table proposals; R5 capability-based bodies and schemes; R4 index-generalizing induction & stretch cases; accumulator and tupling entries; probe 8; probe 11\\
P6 & R6 program and proof together; R7 retrieval; R8 behavioral classes; E2 frontends; E3 receipts & quantified-contract suite; probes 14, 16; reference-solution rank reported for the whole suite\\
P7 & R9 proposal interface with an optional model lane & measured accuracy of proposed carriers; results replayable without the model\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

P1 to P3 are engineering and adaptation and can be scheduled with confidence. P4 is mostly engineering with one validation risk (agreement with the kernel). P5 is where the research risk is concentrated: it should be preceded by the expert consultations of Section~\ref{sec:experts} and by paper prototypes on the thirteen stretch cases, and its gate should be read as a target, not a commitment.

\subsection{Risks}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{46mm}L{50mm}L{52mm}@{}}
\toprule
Risk & Consequence & Mitigation\\
\midrule
\endhead
Cost-ordered search changes which candidates are found first & baseline outcomes stay the same but transcripts change; ranking complaints & gate P2 on identical candidate lists before enabling costs; change costs only with the ranking benchmark in hand\\
Custom evaluator disagrees with the kernel & unsound pruning, lost solutions & conservative fragment; differential testing on every generated program; kernel fallback\\
Carrier enumeration slows solved cases & regression in time, not in score & carriers enter only after the plain fold fails at the current cost bound; carrier refutation before search\\
Definition elaborator too slow for recursion candidates & P5 recursion unusable in 10 s & realize only candidates that pass the contract by interpretation\\
Mathlib-scale index too large or slow to load & retrieval lane unusable interactively & environment-fingerprinted on-disk index; reachability pre-pass limits providers to tens\\
Determinism lost under parallel lanes or learned costs & results not reproducible & rank after all lanes stop; learned costs are a pure function of the query's own search history\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
