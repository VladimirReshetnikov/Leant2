\section{A dependency-ordered implementation and evaluation plan}\label{sec:roadmap}

\subsection{One joint state, several bounded services}

The implementation plans in N1--N9 are consolidated here. They retain the
native kernel boundary and a reference search order while making dependencies,
incomplete results and service costs explicit. Bounded carriers, sketches and
native induction can proceed before unrestricted carrier inference; a
best-first frontier need not precede every improvement. These are dependency
choices, not predictions of speedup.

An explicit node contains the persistent native state, original request ID,
typed program and obligations, universe constraints, recursive frames,
observation read sets, assignment versions, proof jobs, immutable cost and
an expansion cursor. The cursor resumes a rule partway through its alternatives
instead of repeating its earlier prefix. Shared immutable terms and closed
checked facts differ from branch-dependent residuals. Work charges remain
monotone outside rollback. Eviction, spill and fallback policies are resource
decisions, never proofs of exhaustion.

\begin{lstlisting}
while logical work remains and the external deadline permits:
    job := chooseEligibleJobAtDeterministicCheckpoint()
    restore(job.nativeState)
    validateReadDependencies(job)
    reply := advanceOneBoundedServiceAction(job)
    match reply:
      alternatives(xs): enqueueWithStableIds(xs)
      blocked(reads): suspendUntilRelevantChange(job, reads)
      refuted(evidence, scope): validateAndRecordExclusion(evidence, scope)
      proofReady(term, proof): checkFrozenQueryAndRecord(term, proof)
      unknown(reason): retainUnresolvedJob(job, reason)
    invalidateAffectedObservations()
    reserveProofAndPublicationWork()
    chargeWorkOutsideRollbackAndCommitTraceEvent()
return checkedAnswers, publicationReceipts, frontierAndUnresolvedJobs
\end{lstlisting}

This is pseudocode. A service action may contain an expensive Lean primitive,
so reduction, elaboration and external inference need resource boundaries.
Model absence and cancellation are recorded input events. Proposals state
whether they stay within or extend the grammar; old coverage or optimality
claims do not silently extend to a larger language.

The example module names in the incoming reports are organizational suggestions,
not an additional set of features or files to create verbatim. An implementation
may combine responsibilities while preserving their contracts. Detailed
algorithms appear in Part~III; duplicating them here would create competing
definitions of the same state.

\subsection{Phases and exit evidence}

Every implementation phase preserves the maintained regression suite at both
5 s and 10 s, with exact revisions, provider inventories and outcome meanings.
The old six-harness table remains historical. Documentation integration and
finite-model reruns do not satisfy an implementation phase's gates.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{12mm}L{59mm}L{77mm}@{}}
\toprule Phase & Work & Evidence required to leave the phase\\\midrule\endhead
P0 & Freeze request semantics, result distinctions, receipts, identities and complete cost accounting. & Unrelated proofs, wrong universes and forbidden negative axioms are refused; timeout is unknown; certificate and publication replay are separate.\\
P1 & Observation decomposition, closed caches, local proof adapters, indexed induction and useful primitive/provider coverage. & Cache-disabled comparisons preserve decisions; indexed and missing-lemma cases have checked witnesses; open and rollback cases remain safe.\\
P2 & Bounded carrier/motive/strengthening grammar, explicit finishers, finite-state exclusion and local invariants. & Supplied-carrier body search and full invention are separate; failed proposals are charged; finite bounds and original contracts are checked.\\
P3 & Resumable states with original DFS first, then fixed-cost/fair policies and conservative dependency components. & Equal completed logical prefixes before policy changes; frontier memory measured; exhaustive small reference cases lose no valid in-bound witness.\\
P4 & Typed partial evaluation, recursive capabilities, checked realization, local proof jobs and bounded helper/angelic refinement. & Supported-fragment arguments and adversarial tests; interpreter agreement; unresolved survivors retained; universal contracts proved where claimed.\\
P5 & Retrieval and scheduling optimization; optional learned proposals and user-facing ranking. & Matched total-cost experiments, including inference, proof and compilation; held-out families; replay without the model; claims match the objective.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

P1 and bounded portions of P2 may proceed together. P3 is not a prerequisite
for a useful sketch or induction adapter. Stronger pruning in P4 depends on
semantic support rather than solved-count improvements alone. Aspirations such
as sub-millisecond nodes or solving every stretch case remain hypotheses to
measure. E6 propositional countermodels need the contextual bridge in
Section~\ref{sec:semantic-boundaries} before they count as Lean negatives.

\subsection{Benchmark dimensions and accounting}

Record the exact target, contract, policy, providers, grammar, bounds, reference
witness and recursive capabilities. Cross these dimensions where they isolate
a cause: type-only, finite examples and universal property; carrier supplied
versus invented; helpers supplied, retrieved or invented; complete versus
incomplete traces; reference-oracle access versus examples-only input;
supplied versus invented motives and invariants; logical certification versus
executable publication. Oracle and supplied-artifact arms diagnose the
remaining bottleneck and must not be counted as unassisted invention. A
reference establishes representability only in its declared grammar. A helper
with an accumulator does not inherit a complete specification from examples
of the original seed-specific function.

Keep saturated regressions, the thirteen unassisted Church stretch cases,
indexed families, recursive compositions and universal contracts as separate
tracks. Deduplicate imported benchmarks by semantic task family. Report
construction, certified refutation, unification, observation, proof, realization
and retrieval work; first candidate, first certified answer and first executable
publication times; peak memory; reference-equivalent rank; and exact stop
reason. Medians, tails and regressions matter as well as total score. Cold
imports/indexes and warm interactive runs are distinct. Compiler/runtime,
hardware, threads, cache policy and repeated-run variation belong in reports.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{39mm}L{109mm}@{}}
\toprule Ablation & Comparison that identifies the effect\\\midrule\endhead
Observation/cache & Same expansion order: no cache, closed cache, dependency cache; compare decisions and work.\\
Search policy & Resumable DFS before queue heuristics; fixed versus learned dispatch with identical grammar and frozen ranking.\\
Carrier discovery & Supplied carrier, enumeration, feature proposals and full invention; body filling and proof budgets matched.\\
Angelic refinement & Unknown calls remain stuck versus shared typed constraints; count failed guesses and unresolved domains.\\
Proof construction & Closed post-hoc portfolio versus local invariants and induction-aligned verification conditions.\\
Retrieval & Curated baseline, ranking-only retrieval and justified bounded filtering; report missed usable components.\\
Learned proposals & Model-free, recorded/shuffled proposals, fixed-grammar guidance and explicit grammar extension as separate arms.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Adversarial tests before stronger claims}

Each pruning test checks both the survivor's original gate and preservation of
known valid bounded completions. Positive certification alone misses false
negative search results. The repeated source checklists become one matrix:

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{40mm}L{108mm}@{}}
\toprule Test & Required behavior\\\midrule\endhead
Dependency closure & Shared type/universe variables, local lets, dictionaries or relational observations prevent unsound factorization despite different printed targets.\\
Rollback and delayed holes & Assignment--rollback--reassignment, identifier reuse and different closure contexts invalidate stale residuals; irrelevant changes permit valid reuse.\\
Samples and finite state & Missing entries are not differences; pairwise compatibility is not transitive; enumerated values are not a carrier upper bound.\\
Parametricity/equality & Unlawful \code{BEq}, computational dictionaries and uncertified providers do not silently acquire naturality or propositional-equality laws.\\
Recursive constraints & Equal calls share results; failed angelic choices leave alternatives; a guessed finite domain cannot refute a larger one without coverage.\\
Dependent realization & Casts remain typed; decrease obligations and interpreter agreement replay; insufficient fuel is unknown.\\
Program and proof & Proofs may constrain open values; inadequate invariants refute the program--invariant pair only; proof jobs receive fair service.\\
Original request & Weaker contracts, specialized universes, unrelated proof types and forbidden positive or negative axioms cannot replace the requested result.\\
Frontier and rank & Evicted/unresolved states remain visible; an incumbent is not optimal while a pending lower bound permits a better answer.\\
External proposals & Escaped locals, unauthorized dependencies, oversized syntax and environment changes are refused or bounded; raw and parsed proposals are retained.\\
Publication/replay & Certification and compilation status differ; speed-perturbed workers reproduce logical checkpoints, not necessarily equal timed answer sets.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Risks and falsifiable decisions}

Frontiers exchange repeated work for retained state; measure memory before
blaming snapshots alone. Expressive carriers can slow easy cases, so retain
a fair plain-grammar path and charge proposal failures. A custom evaluator
needs a supported-fragment conservativity argument; closed-program agreement
is a test, not that argument. Interpreted behavior guides realization only
through its documented connection to the checked definition. Retrieval indexes
need environment identities and cold-loading measurements. Deterministic
epochs and receipts replace the claim that sorting after parallel deadlines
makes discovery deterministic. Continue research phases based on these
measurements and obligations, not novelty, shorter terms or plausible model
suggestions alone.
